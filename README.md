# Nifty IV Event Study — RBI Policy Dates 2024-2025

## Research Question
Does Nifty implied volatility consistently spike before RBI policy 
announcements and crush after?

## Key Finding
IV behavior around RBI dates is NOT uniform.
It depends on whether the outcome was expected or surprising.

## Method
- Calculated 10-day Historical Volatility from Nifty 50 price data
- Analyzed HV behavior 20 days before and after each RBI date
- 8 RBI meetings studied: Feb 2024 to Apr 2025

## Main Conclusions
- Uncertain outcomes = IV spike before, crush after
- Expected outcomes = muted IV response
- Feb 2025 rate cut (first in years) = POST-event IV spike due to yield curve repricing
- Jun 2024 = double uncertainty (election + RBI) = largest IV spike

## Trading Implication
Selling options before RBI when outcome is highly expected captures IV crush.
Avoid short volatility when macro backdrop is uncertain.
